Fresnel Hero

All controls are on the stage: drag a fixture's centre circle to aim, drag its barn-door knobs to shape the beam, tap the color dots to relight it, and use the + / − circles on the truss to add or remove lights. Light each performer so the goal % of their silhouette matches each target color — overlapping beams mix additively.

Drag the centre circle on a fixture to aim · drag the barn-door flap knobs to shape the beam · + adds a light, − removes one
Color mixing key: Red Green Blue Red+Green = Yellow Red+Blue = Magenta Green+Blue = Cyan All three = White No light at all = Shadow
Tip: barn doors stop at the center of the beam, so closing both narrows a lamp to a sliver but never kills it outright — handy for keeping a color off a performer who doesn't need it.
